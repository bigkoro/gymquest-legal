Privacy Policy

GymQuest · last updated 13 August 2026

GymQuest builds a workout from the equipment and minutes you have today, and turns it into an RPG quest. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of our own: your profile, workout history, streak and character all live on your device. This page describes exactly what stays on the device, what leaves it, and who receives it.

Data that never leaves your device

The app stores the following in local storage on your phone. We cannot read it, and it is not backed up to any server we operate:

  • Your profile: goal, experience level, body weight and height, available equipment, training days per week.
  • Every workout you generate and complete, including the sets, reps and weights you log.
  • Your character: level, XP, streak, badges, boss progress.
  • App settings: language, sound, and the hour your daily reminder fires.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. Because there is no account, this data cannot be recovered or transferred to a new phone by us.

Data that is sent to third parties

Who What Why
Google Analytics 4 Anonymous usage events (app opened, onboarding finished, quest started and completed, paywall viewed, purchase tapped, share card opened, reminder set, language changed) together with a random analytics identifier, approximate location derived from IP, device model and OS version. No name, email, body measurements or workout log is sent. To see which parts of the app get used, so we know what to improve.
RevenueCat A random app user ID and your subscription status, plus the receipt data Apple returns for a purchase. To unlock GymQuest Pro on your device and keep it unlocked across reinstalls and restores.
Apple Payment and receipt handling for in-app purchases, and delivery of the app itself. Apple processes all purchases; we never see your payment details.

That is the complete list. There is no advertising SDK, no ad tracking, no third-party data broker, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a single daily reminder locally on your device, at the hour you train most often or an hour you pick. Nothing is sent to a push server. You can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Health data

GymQuest does not read from or write to Apple Health. The body weight and height you enter are used only to estimate calories and pick sensible starting loads, and they stay on the device.

Children

GymQuest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off analytics collection for your device by disabling app tracking in iOS Settings, or delete the app.
  • Delete all local data by deleting the app.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • To ask what we hold about you or to request deletion, email the address below. Because there is no account, we will normally need the RevenueCat app user ID shown in the app's settings to find anything at all.
